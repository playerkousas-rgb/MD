### 申請須知 **Application Guidance** 

1. 申請表格必須於活動截止日期前／考驗舉行前2個月連同計劃書（如適用）遞交九龍柯士甸道童軍徑香港童軍中心9樓青少年活動署，逾期 遞交有關資料及未備妥所需簽署之申請，將不獲處理。 

Applicant should submit this form together with a copy of the project plan (if applicable) to Programme Branch, 9/F, Hong Kong Scout Centre, Scout Path, Austin Road, Kowloon before the activity’s application deadline / 2 months prior to the award assessment. Late application, application with insufficient information and / or signature(s) will not be entertained. 

2. 青少年活動署在收到申請表後，將按需要邀請 閣下提供有助審批的相關資料，或進行面試。 After receiving the application form, Programme Branch will invite the applicant to provide relevant materials that are helpful for approval, or attend an interview if necessary. 

3. 申請結果均以書面或電郵通知，故請清楚及正確填寫各項聯絡資料。若 閣下於活動截止報名日期後／考驗活動舉行前 1個月仍未收到通 知，請與青少年活動署職員聯絡（電話：2957 6414）。 

Applicant will be informed in writing of the application result.  Thus, please fill in all contact information correctly. If you do not receive any acknowledgement 1 month after the activity’s application deadline / before the commencement of the assessment, please contact Programme Branch staff at 2957 6414. 

4. 申請表格內填報的個人及其他有關資料，只供本會處理申請本津貼及有關用途。在表格內提供的個人及其他有關資料純屬自願。然而，如 果沒有正確或足夠的資料，本會可能無法處理有關的申請。 

The personal data and other related information provided in the application form will be used by the Association for dealing with the application for participating in this Subsidy Scheme and other related purposes.  The provision of personal data and other related information by means of this application form is voluntary.  However, we may not be able to process the application if no accurate or adequate data is provided. 

5. 在一般情況下，申請表將於審批完成後2年銷毀。 Application form will normally be destroyed 2 years after completion of the application examination.
