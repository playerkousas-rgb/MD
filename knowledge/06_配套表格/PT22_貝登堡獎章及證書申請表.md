## 貝登堡獎章證書申請辦法
樂行童軍於完成貝登堡獎章考驗的各項活動後，應：

1. 填妥本表格及在 (3) 申請人簽署一欄簽署，然後交由樂行童軍團長簽署 (4) 提名欄。
2. 將表格呈交區總監及地域總監簽署 (5) 推薦欄。
3. 本表格連同紀錄冊內有關個人資料、有效日期、貝登堡獎章考驗的頁次及各項有關證書之影印本，經地域轉交總會青少年活動署。
4. 青少年活動總監核實所有資料後，即頒發獎章及證書。
5. 全年均接受申請，申請如在每年 9 月 15 日或以前呈交總會青少年活動署，其證書將安排於該年之香港童軍大會操中頒發。於該日後所遞交之申請，其證書則留待於下年度之香港童軍大會操中頒發。

A Rover Scout, after completing the requirements of the Baden-Powell Award, shall:
(1) Complete this form (PT22) and sign in Column 3 (Applicant's Signature) and submit it to Rover Scout Leader to endorse Column 4 (Nomination).
(2) Submit the form to District Commissioner then to Regional Commissioner for endorsement in Column 5 (Recommendation).
(3) Submit the completed form together with a photocopy of the following documents to the Programme Branch via Regional Office: information regarding personal details, valid date and the achievement pages of the Baden-Powell Award in the Rover Scout Record Book as well as all relevant certificates.
(4) Upon approval by the Programme Commissioner, the Award and Certificate will be issued.
(5) The Award application is open all year round. All applications received by the Programme Branch on or before 15 September each year will have the certificates awarded in the Scout Rally of the same year. All applications received after 15 September will have the certificates awarded in the Scout Rally of the following year.

The provision of personal data and other related information by means of the application form is voluntary. However, if you do not provide adequate and accurate data we may not be able to process your application.
2. 在一般情況下，申請表將於審批完成後 2 年銷毀。Application form will normally be destroyed 2 years after completion of the application examination.
