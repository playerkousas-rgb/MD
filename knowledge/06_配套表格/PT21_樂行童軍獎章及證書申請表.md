## 樂行童軍獎章及證書申請程序 Application procedures
樂行童軍於完成樂行童軍獎章考核的各項活動後，應：

1. 填妥本表格及在 (3) 申請人簽署一欄簽署，然後交由樂行童軍團長簽署 (4) 提名欄。
2. 將表格交由區會助理區總監（樂行童軍）／樂行童軍區長／區指定人士以簽署 (5) 推薦欄，之後交由地域總部總監（樂行童軍）簽署 (6) 批核欄。
3. 本表格連同紀錄冊內有關個人資料、有效日期、樂行童軍獎章考核的頁次及各項有關證書之影印本，經地域轉交總會青少年活動署。
4. 青少年活動署總監核對無誤後，即頒發獎章及證書。

A Rover Scout, after completing the assessment requirement of the Rover Scout Award, shall:
(1) Complete this form and sign in Column 3 (Applicant's Signature) then submit to Rover Scout Leader to endorse Column 4 (Nomination).
(2) Submit to the District to endorse Column 5 (Recommendation) by ADC (Rover Scouts) / DRSL / District Assigned Person, then submit to Regional Headquarters Commissioner (Rover Scouts) to endorse Column 6 (Approval).
(3) Submit the completed form together with a photocopy of the following documents to the Programme Branch via Regional Office: information regarding personal particulars, valid date and the achievement pages of the Rover Scout Award in the Rover Scout Record Book as well as all relevant certificates.
(4) Upon confirmation by Programme Commissioner, the Award and Certificate will be issued.
