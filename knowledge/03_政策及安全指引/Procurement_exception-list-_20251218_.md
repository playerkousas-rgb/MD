# **SCOUT ASSOCIATION OF HONG KONG** 

# **Exception List of Goods and Services Procurement (Updated in Dec 2025):** 

# Quotations may be dispensed of: 

- A. where goods or services are provided by B P International House, Scout Units, Hong Kong SAR Government, Non-Government Organization Camps/Centres subvented by Government. 

- B. where Chinese-style meal reception procured for the Annual General Meeting/ Inauguration Ceremony/Anniversary event of Scout Unit is at a unit price (per 12-person table) not higher than the charge for similar set menu provided by Commissioners’ Club. 

- C. where total sum* of goods or services is HK$500 or below. 

- D. where goods or services, of total sum* not exceeding HK$2,000, are procured from the suppliers/providers listed in Table 1.
