序 **Preface** 

深資童軍支部是青少年邁向成熟的重要學習平臺。15 至20 歲的青年正 處於確立價值觀、探索人生方向的關鍵階段，適合透過更高自主度及責 任感的訓練模式，學習自我管理、策劃及領導他人。此階段的訓練有助 青年逐步建立清晰的個人目標與人生方向。 

深資童軍支部以「自務自治．明日領袖」為核心理念，鼓勵成員在自務 自治的架構下，主動策劃活動、參與服務及接受挑戰，從中鍛煉組織能 力、溝通技巧、決策能力及承擔精神，為成為青年領袖作好準備。成員 在實踐中學習為決定負責，培養成熟的領導態度。 

是次修訂的訓練綱要，清晰整合段章制度與進度性學習歷程，強調策劃、 行動與反思並重，協助深資童軍建立成熟的思考模式及穩健的行事態度， 並培養持續自我提升的能力。整體設計更着重學習深度與質素。 

新訓練綱要亦加強深資童軍與社區的連結，透過實際服務與團隊合作， 引導成員關心社會需要，在真實情境中實踐所學，培養公民責任感與社 會承擔。讓服務不只是活動，而成為價值實踐的一部分。 

本人謹向支部訓練檢討工作小組及所有參與修訂工作的領袖致謝，感謝 他們為完善深資童軍訓練制度所作出的寶貴貢獻。期望深資童軍能善用 童軍提供的學習平臺，勇於承擔、持續成長，逐步發展成為具影響力的 青年領袖。

#### 前  言 **Foreword** 

在整個童軍運動中，深資童軍支部將涵括15 至20 歲（即21 歲生日前 一天）的成員，在這一階段的青少年成員，往往面對各種困難。由學業 環境急速轉變，從高中至面對公開試到大專/ 大學或職場的過渡，帶來 巨大不確定性；同時，他們需從青少年逐漸蛻變為成年人，面對社會責 任、身份轉變及生活平衡等的挑戰，正正就是本綱要提供的學習需要： 

本綱要主要修訂如下： 

1. 強化「自務自治」的角色。特此修改現時的「深資童軍執行委員會 訓練班」，除了讓成員明瞭「執行委員會」、「有效會議程序」， 更有系統地「組織活動」。同時讓成員建立人際網絡，共同面對挑戰。 

2. 強化「選擇」。作為青少年成員最高規格之獎章，除保存傳統「童 軍技能」的訓練外，亦於不同範疇中增加「新選項」，例如：「服 務社會」項目中新增三個選項（手語、精神健康急救、SOUL Keeper 精神健康守護者課程）及「多元技能」項目中的「新體驗」等，提 供更廣闊的選擇。 

期望深資童軍成員在新修訂的訓練綱要下，善用寶貴的時間獲得最有效 的訓練，在個人發展的同時，亦能為童軍運動及社會發展出一分力。最 後，本人在此代表香港童軍總會，向為編制本訓練綱要付出努力和貢獻 的人士致謝。

#### 引  言 

#### **Introduction** 

本書詳述深資童軍支部組織、規條及訓練課程，並詳列深資童軍支部各 個獎章的考驗範圍及應達到的水平。 

本書乃本會正式訓練手冊之一，有關領袖均須遵照執行。如有任何進一 步修訂，請留意日後所發出之通告。 

###### **何謂自治?** 

自治為透過民主選舉制度並根據童軍運動原則，以執行委員會模式自行 管理及發展團務。 

###### **何謂自務?** 

自務為成員按照該旅團的需要自行計劃深資童軍團行事曆，積極參與訓 練、服務及各類型比賽，使之具備組織能力，能擔當成年人角色。 自治和自務兩者必須互相配合纔可全面發展成員的個性，最終目的是讓 深資童軍累積更多以童軍活動為本的管理經驗，培養領袖素質。 

###### **領袖角色** 

領袖作為成員的楷模，須定時給予執行委員會成員及其他工作小組成員 指導。有關引導工作包括活動前的計劃建議及活動後的解說及檢討。惟 領袖需給予成員執行計劃的自由度，以發展成員之領導能力為目標。

###### **主考人角色** 

一般簽發人均可擔任為主考人。而簽發人亦可安排由相關資格的人士擔 任主考人。主考人可以是相關範疇中專業人士，並不一定為童軍領袖， 唯簽發人需要為主考人提供清晰及適當的指示以便完成考覈。

#### 第一章 深資童軍支部的組織及規條 **CHAPTER 1: Organization and Rules of Venture Scout Section** 

###### **目的** 

深資童軍支部旨在延續童軍支部之訓練，使童軍運動得以薪火相傳，並 為社會培育未來領袖。 

深資童軍支部的教育目標是「自務自治 明日領袖」。深資童軍採用自務 自治方式，並透過戶外生活、自我挑戰活動及遞進式訓練，學習自學、 自立及自律、鍛煉良好體魄、培養策劃、組織及領導才能、領略與人共 事之道、增進人際關係技巧、實踐團隊精神及為踏入社會前做好準備。 

###### **方法** 

在成人領袖的引導下，深資童軍運用執行委員會制度，以自治和自務方 式配合有進度性及富挑戰性之活動及訓練誘導成員，以達到深資童軍支 部的目的。 

###### **童軍誓詞** 

我願以信譽為誓，竭盡所能； 對神明，對國家，盡責任； 對別人，要幫助； 對規律，必遵行。 

###### **童軍規律** 

- （一） 童軍信用為人敬。 

- （二） 童軍待人要忠誠。 

- （三） 童軍友善兼親切。 

- （四） 童軍相處如手足。 

- （五） 童軍勇敢不怕難。 

- （六） 童軍愛物更惜陰。 

- （七） 童軍自重又重人。

###### **年齡規限** 

深資童軍支部的年齡規限是15 至20 歲。於年滿21 歲當日，其深資童軍身 分將自動結束。 

###### **深資童軍團的最低應有標準** 

根據總會《政策組織及規條》： 

###### **人數** 

一已成立的深資童軍團，最少要有深資童軍六名。 

###### **領袖** 

一深資童軍團最少要有兩名領袖，其中一名必須持有領袖委任書。此外， 其中一名領袖的性別必須與該團成員相同。如團內有男及女成員，則必 須有男及女領袖。 

###### **訓練** 

深資童軍的活動與訓練，必須遵照總會出版的「深資童軍訓練綱要」及 所頒佈的任何有關修訂文件。 

###### **團執行委員會** 

該委員會負責設計及推行年度活動計劃。 

###### **深資童軍團內部組織及功能 內部組織** 

###### **1. 全體團員大會** 

1.1 職責 

此大會為深資童軍團之最高行政機構，負責訂定及修改團章、 制定長遠團務政策、選出執行委員會委員、檢視執行委員及其 他臨時工作小組所籌劃之活動與訓練及審核團財務情況。此大 會每年最少召開一次會議。 

- 1.2  組成 

所有有效深資童軍成員。 

- 1.3  權力 

所有有效深資童軍成員可透過民主及公平的投票方法就特定議 題投票。除非有關議決違背童軍運動原則，否則所有有關團務 的投票結果均屬最後決定。

###### **2. 執行委員會** 

###### 2.1 職責 

執行委員會須遵照團員大會的決定負責團內的組織、活動及行政 等常務事宜。短期計劃應由執行委員會首先策動。 

###### 2.2 組成 

執行委員會設有主席、文書、財務及其他職位，由全體團員大會 視乎情況而定。執行委員會由所有團員選舉產生，包括主席及其 他由團員決定的委員人選。各職位可在團員大會中選出或由各委 員互選擔任。不論採用簡單多數制、內閣制、兩輪決選制、累積 投票制或比例制等選舉辦法，逐個職位投票或選出委員再決定職 位，選舉制度必須因應團的發展而訂，而選舉辦法應清晰地載於 團章。 

###### 2.3 權力 

全體團員大會及團章賦予執行委員會及其委員權力代表深資 童軍團處理一般行政及常務事宜。但如有議案涉及整個團利 益，相關事項必須於全體團員大會中詳細討論及投票表決。 

###### **3. 臨時工作小組** 

###### 3.1 職責 

小組主要用作推行旅團的特別活動。職責應由執行委員會決定。 3.2 組成 

臨時小組成員由執行委員會委任或由團員自由加入。較多成員的 深資童軍團可通過成立這種小組，讓非執行委員會委員的深資 童軍亦可參加實際團務及活動策劃，讓各團員都有機會發揮領導 才。臨時小組主席及其他工作崗位由該組組員互選或協商產生。 

###### 3.3 權力 

由於臨時工作小組由執行委員會委任，故需直接向執行委員會匯 報工作並向全體團員負責。

###### **領袖的職責及要求** 

###### **4. 領袖角色** 

- 4.1 深資童軍團領袖在全體團員大會中可擔當顧問角色，向各深資童 軍成員提供意見。 

- 4.2 深資童軍團領袖皆為執行委員會的當然委員。深資童軍團長可根 據團員之成熟程度及經驗，出任執行委員會主席，或安排團內領 袖擔任執行委員會任何職位。 

- 4.3 深資童軍團長作為團之最高負責人，擁有團內任何會議所通過的 議案之最終解釋權及否決權。如團內任何會議之議決違背童軍運 動原則，深資童軍團長有責任及權力否決有關投票結果並停止相 關活動，並向團員作出適當之指引。 

###### **預期成果** 

深資童軍應積極參與或策劃各類型活動（如技能訓練、服務、比賽等）， 出任團執行委員會、工作小組成員及青少年活動署和各地域的深資童軍 議會委員，並嘗試於各類型活動中擔當領導角色。當深資童軍完成整個 支部訓練後，應可達致下列預期成果︰ 

1. 深造童軍技能外，啟發「多元技能」發展； 

2. 掌握「活動策劃」的技巧，培育「明日領袖」； 

3. 拓闊人際網絡，建立合作精神； 

4. 培養「自務自治」及「服務社會」的精神； 

5. 享受「戶外探險」，拓展視野，從而欣賞及尊重大自然。 

當完成深資童軍訓練或年滿21 歲（以西曆生日計），深資童軍可繼續從 事童軍活動，擔任童軍領袖工作；或加入樂行童軍團。樂行童軍是第五 個訓練支部，旨在維繫關懷社會、熱心服務之青年，給予他們機會及鼓 勵，為童軍運動及社會服務。

# 深資童軍段章設計概念 

###### 活動策劃 

活動策劃段章旨在培養成員的活動策劃及團隊合作 能力。段章中兩人合作砌拼圖是象徵與他人協作的 精神；而拼圖則代表每位成員在項目中都扮演著獨 特而重要的角色，強調有效溝通與協作的重要性。 

###### 社會服務 

社會服務段章旨在培養成員幫助他人的精神。 段章中人與人之間互相擁抱，象徵世界大同；而左 手掌與心形的結合則體現童軍對社區的奉獻和愛 心。此段章鼓勵成員積極參與社會服務，展現對社 會的責任感。 

###### 多元技能 

多元技能段章旨在鼓勵成員多元發展，探索和深造 包括童軍技能在內的各項技能。 段章中薪火的元素象徵探索未知領域的熱情，同時 也代表童軍傳統技能的承傳，而燈泡則寓意著擴闊 視野及探索世界。 

###### 戶外探險 

戶外探險段章旨在鼓勵成員通過戶外活動挑戰自 我，欣賞和尊重大自然。 段章中的指南針象徵引領與探索的精神，幫助成員 在旅途中找到方向；指針指向山峯，寓意成員勇於 挑戰。而在外框的北極星指引下，成員能在冒險中 堅持信念，辨認方向，向前邁進。

#### 甚麼是「有目的之旅程」及自選事工指引 **What is a "Journey with Purpose" and the Guidelines for Self-Selected Projects** 

1. 「有目的之旅程」（Journey with Purpose）是為了實現某種特定的 目標或意義。這戶外／境外進行的旅程可以包括學習新技能、深入 瞭解某種文化、參與志願活動，或是自我反思與成長。通過不同方 式的旅程，深資童軍能在享受旅程的同時，獲得更深刻的體驗和個 人發展。 

2. 自選旅程事工項目應必須以徒步、單車、自行駕駛、機動化交通工 具或／及海上形式前往有關地點進行相關事工；或相關事工應須於 郊外／戶外／境外進行。因現時科技發達，自選事工不應單純透過 網絡資源便可完成，避免事工項目變為「為做而做」，違背進行「遠 足旅程」之原則。 

3. 自選事工是以「專題研究、文化探索、服務或活動考察等」為大方向， 強調「須對個人、社會或童軍運動具意義及貢獻」的原則。例子包 括但不限於： 

   - 特定學習主題（專題研究） 例如：西貢東壩一帶的岩石研究、大埔谷埔一帶的紅樹林研究、 米埔一帶的生物多樣性研究或者樹木普查、大欖一帶的廟宇研 究、水質研究、珊瑚研究等。 

   - 服務 

      - 例如：檢查山徑標距柱狀況、檢查需要山徑維修或需清理排水道 的狀況等、向行山人士介紹及分享「山野不留痕」的原則等。 

   - 活動或文化考察 例如：為幼童軍或童軍支部成員設計及實地考察一天遠足路線、 香港歷史遊蹤（如：探索及分享維多利亞城界石、麥景陶碉堡7 座碉堡）等。

###### **Introduction** 

This book sets out the organisation, rules and training scheme of the Venture Scout Section. It also details the assessment requirements and standards of various Awards. 

This is an official Training Handbook of Scout Association of Hong Kong. Its contents must be closely observed by all leaders. Any further amendments will be announced by circular. 

###### **What is self-governing?** 

Self-governing means the Venture Scout Unit and Unit affairs are selfmanaged by the Unit Executive Committee based on a democratic electoral system and the principles of Scouting. 

###### **What is self-programming?** 

Self-programming means the Unit members devise their own calendar based on the needs of the Unit, take active participation in training, service and all kinds of competitions, so that they are recognised as capable of organising and of playing an adult role in society. 

Self-governing and self-programming must be applied in coordination so as to develop the characteristics and personalities of Venture Scouts in a comprehensive way. The ultimate goal is to let Venture Scouts accumulate more Scouting-based management experience and to nurture the essential qualities of a leader. 

###### **Role of Leaders** 

Leaders are role models of Unit members. They should provide guidance for members of the Executive Committee and other working groups. Related guidance includes planning and suggestions before an activity, and debriefi ng and refl ection after the activity. However, leaders should give freedom to members to implement their plans so that they can help develop members’ leadership skills.

###### **Role of Issuing Persons of Badges** 

Issuing persons may sign and issue valid certificates for badges. They may enlist the help of a relevant assessor in confirming the completion of various requirements. 

- Membership Badge: Membership badge is awarded by the Venture Scout Leader after consulting the Unit Executive Committee. 

- Venture Scout Epaulettes: Venture scout epaulettes are awarded by the Venture Scout Leader and may assign an assessor to oversee the completion of requirements. 

- Badges: Badges are awarded by the Venture Scout Leader. Venture Scout Leaders may assign appropriate assessors to oversee requirements of badges. ###### **CHAPTER 1: Organization and Rules of  the Venture Scout Section** 

###### **The Aim** 

The aim of the Venture Scout Section is to continue the progressive training of the Scout Section in order to pass the torch to the new generation in the Scout Movement, and nurture future leaders for our society. 

The educational goal of the Venture Scout Section is "Self-governing and Self-programming, Tomorrow's Leaders". 

Venture Scouts adopt a self-governing and self-programming approach. Through life outdoors, challenging themselves, and progressive training, they learn self-study, self-reliance, and selfdiscipline; forge physical fitness; foster planning, organisational, and leadership skills; embrace teamwork; enhance interpersonal skills; practise team spirit; and prepare themselves to take a constructive place in society. 

###### **The Method** 

Guided by adult leaders, Venture Scouts apply the Executive Committee system. Through self-governing and self-programming, Venture Scout Units shall have progressive and challenging activities and training to achieve the Aim of the Venture Scout Section. 

###### **The Scout Promise** 

On my honour, I promise that I will do my best To do my duty to God and to my Country To help other people And to keep the Scout Law.

- **Internal Organisation of a Venture Scout Unit** 1. General Meeting 

   - 1.1 Responsibility 

The General Meeting is the highest administration authority of a Venture Scout Unit. The General Meeting can write and amend the Unit Constitution, formulate long-term plans for the Unit, elect Executive Committee members, review the programme and training planned by the Executive Committee and other temporary working groups, and audit the financial position of the Unit. The General Meeting should be convened at least once a year.

<!-- p.80 -->
###### 1.2 Composition 

All valid Venture Scout members. 

###### 1.3 Authority 

All valid Venture Scout members can vote for specific issues via a democratic and fair voting system. Unless the results violate the principles of Scouting, all voting related to Unit affairs will be considered final. 

###### 2. Executive Committee 

###### 2.1 Responsibility 

The Unit Executive Committee is responsible for the composition, organisation, programme, training, and administration of the Unit as decided in the General Meeting. Short-term planning should be initiated by the Executive Committee in the first instance. 

###### 2.2 Composition: 

An Executive Committee should have a Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer and other positions as deemed appropriate, depending on the results of the General Meeting and the size of the Unit. The Executive Committee members, including the Chairperson and other members, shall be elected by the Unit members. Positions on the Executive Committee can be elected during the General Meeting or within the Executive Committee. There can be various election methods, e.g. plurality voting system, parliamentary system, two-round system, cumulative voting system, and proportional representation. Elections can be done position by position or by electing the committee members first before deciding the allocation of positions. No matter which voting system is used, it should be determined in accordance with the Unit’s situation. The election method should be clearly stated in the Unit Constitution as well. 

###### 2.3 Authority 

The General Meeting and the Unit Constitution empower the Executive Committee and its members to handle administrative and general affairs of the Unit. However, if there exists any motion that is related or of interest to the whole membership of the Unit, all related matters must be discussed and voted on during the General Meeting.

   - 3.1 Responsibility 

Temporary working groups are mainly responsible for implementing special activities of the Unit. The exact job duties shall be decided by the Executive Committee. 

- 3.2 Composition 

Members of the temporary working groups are Unit members who join voluntarily or are appointed by the Executive Committee. Venture Scout Units with more members can let non-Executive Committee members participate in Unit affairs and activity planning through temporary working groups, so that Venture Scouts have the opportunities to develop their leadership. The Chairperson and other positions of the temporary working groups are decided by the group members through election or appointment. 

- 3.3 Authority 

Since temporary working groups are appointed by the Executive Committee, group members report directly to the Executive Committee and are responsible to all Unit members. 

###### **Responsibilities and Requirement of Leaders** 

4. Role of Leaders 

   - 4.1 Leaders of a Venture Scout Unit are advisors in the General Meeting and shall provide opinions for Unit members. 

   - 4.2 All leaders of a Venture Scout Unit are ex-officio members of the Unit Executive Committee. The Venture Scout Leader, depending on the maturity and experience of the Unit members, may choose to be the Chairman of the Unit Executive Committee, or assign any Unit leaders to take up any positions in the Executive Committee. 

   - 4.3 The Venture Scout Leader is the Unit person-in-charge with the highest authority. He/ she possesses the right to interpret and the power to veto motions passed during meetings within the Unit. If any decisions made during meetings within the Unit violate the principles of Scouting, the Venture Scout Leader has the responsibility and the power to veto relevant voting results and terminate the related activities.

###### **What is a "Journey with Purpose" and the Guidelines for Self-Selected Projects** 

1. An expedition is a “journey with a purpose”, meaning it is designed to achieve a goal or have specific meaning to the team. This journey, conducted outdoors or overseas, can involve learning and applying new skills, gaining a deep understanding of a culture, performing volunteer service, or engaging in selfreflection and growth. While enjoying different types of journeys, Venture Scouts gain profound experiences and personal development. 

2.  Expedition projects must be conducted along the journey, whether on foot, bicycle, driving, mechanical transport, or by sea. This can involve travelling to reach relevant locations to carry out the project, as the outdoors and overseas are exciting places for exploration. While many projects can be completed online, expedition projects should not be completed merely through online resources, nor done simply for the sake of fulfilling a requirement. 

3. Expedition projects can take the broad direction of field research, exploring cultures, service, or preparing for future activities, etc., emphasising the principle that they "must be meaningful and beneficial to individuals, society, or the Scout Movement. Examples are not limited to: 

   - Field Research For example: Research on the rocks around the East Dam of High Island Reservoir in Sai Kung, research on the mangroves around Kuk Po in Tai Po, research on biodiversity or tree surveys around Mai Po, research on temples around Tai Lam, water quality studies, coral research, etc. 

   - Service For example: Inspecting the condition of distance posts on trails, checking for trails that need maintenance or drains that need clearing, introducing and sharing "Leave No Trace" principles with hikers, etc. 

   - Activity Preparation or Cultural Exploration For example: Designing and reconnoitering a one-day hiking route for Cub Scouts or Scouts, Hong Kong historical trail exploration (e.g., exploring and sharing findings on the City of Victoria Boundary Stones, the seven MacIntosh Forts), etc.

It aims to give Rover Scouts a basic understanding of the Section and training scheme, guiding members to experience and understand their Rover journey. It allows them to experience Scout activities, learn basic Scoutcraft, and serves as a way for Rover Scouts from different backgrounds to interact with each other.

###### **Community Involvement Badge** 

The Community Involvement Badge aims to develop the civic awareness of Venture Scouts by community education and involvement in service projects. Venture Scouts learn to recognise the needs and problems in the society, understand the policies related to daily life, and get involved practically in community affairs. It is hoped that Venture Scouts will develop further civic responsibility, contributing to a better society. 

The Community Involvement Badge consists of two parts: (A) Thematic Workshop and (B) Practical Application. 

The Thematic Workshops centre on the following 7 themes: 

- (1) Environmental Hygiene and Cleanliness 

- (2) Anti-corruption 

- (3) Fight Against Crime 

- (4) Drug Education 

- (5) Equal Opportunities 

- (6) AIDS Prevention Education 

- (7) Respect for Intellectual Property Rights

###### **Assessment Requirements:** 

Participants must complete Part (A) and Part (B) within six months: 

###### (A) 

Complete at least one thematic study course recognised by the Association, and be able to: 

1.  State and explain knowledge related to the chosen theme, and list the appropriate skills and attitude; and 

2.  Explain the aims, meanings, methods and modes of community involvement, and 

3.  Describe effective ways of planning and organising community involvement activities. 

###### (B) 

Complete a practical application from the selected workshop topic, and meet the following requirements: 

1.  Submit a logbook of the chosen theme that contains at least 3 related newspaper clippings, followed by personal comments and feasible recommendations, 

2.  Complete a thematic study assigned by the Course Leader as a group, and present and lead a discussion on the study with the course or the public; 

3. Work as a team to assist in implementing an activity organised by the HKSAR Government with the consent of the Course Leader, or organise and implement a Service Badge project, submitting plans and reports to the assessor, and 

4.  Participate in large-scale promotional campaigns implemented by the HKSAR Government (applicable to specific topics only).
